## What sharing costs

B01 keeps effort near the separate originals, while all positions stay the same when switching between Goti and Jhor. On the equal mean of three previously examined held-out sources, model-optimal fingering:

| Change against original same-style arrangement | Goti style | Jhor style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Modeled effort | +0.608% | +0.447% |
| Within-word same-finger distance | -0.107% | +1.351% |
| Cross-space same-finger distance | +25.750% | +3.923% |

One arrangement to remember is the intended benefit. Goti's larger cross-space cost and the relearning needed from both original maps are real trade-offs. Learning, speed and comfort have not been measured. [Why we chose B01 provisionally](SHARED_LAYOUT.md) and [movement checks](MECHANICS.md) explain the limits.

The [original experimental report](../results/evaluator-e1/run-v1/REPORT.md) remains historical evidence about the original separate arrangements; its results are not B01's current diagnostics.
