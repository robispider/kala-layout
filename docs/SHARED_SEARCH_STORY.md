# Historical shared map: search provenance

## How four billion search proposals led to Kala Layout

We searched for one arrangement of 52 symbols on 26 letter keys, base and Shift, that could serve both Goti and Jhor. The search used the original equal-weight Wikipedia/newspaper training mix and checked modeled effort and movement in each style.

1. **Explore placements:** 20,000 starts across two tracks, each with 200,000 proposed annealing moves—4 billion proposals in total, plus local search.
2. **Screen and verify:** 113,550 distinct arrangements were retained. Final screening and refinement fully scored 961 qualifying maps, including cross-space movement and key-order bounds.
3. **Compare the finalists:** two leading trade-offs remained. We provisionally chose the one that favors Goti's score while retaining the leading Jhor trade-off, accepting the [costs of sharing](SHARED_LAYOUT.md).

The public name is **Kala Layout**. **B01** is its technical research label, retained in reports for traceability. Four billion proposals can revisit arrangements: this is not four billion unique combinations, an exhaustive search, or a proof of optimality. [Search evidence and method](HOW_WE_FOUND_THEM.md), [original run interpretation](../results/shared-layout-relaxed-v1/interpretation-4b-v1/REPORT.md).

Goti builds on the modern fixed-layout entry tradition of **Ekushey's UniJoy and Avro/OmicronLab**. Jhor uses the project's specified context/repeat rule; context-sensitive vowels themselves have prior art in Avro. [Typing-style heritage and credit](TYPING_STYLES.md).
