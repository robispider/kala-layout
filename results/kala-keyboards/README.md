## How the layouts were found

Bangla-DH Pure applies the Colemak Mod-DH effort model (how far each key is, which finger presses it, same-finger and awkward key pairs, and Shift) to Bangla. The computer arranged 52 Bangla symbols on 52 key positions, a space of 8.07 × 10^67 possible layouts, with 512 independent searches of 1,000,000 moves each: more than 512 million candidate layouts scored. It learned from 557,890 Wikipedia and newspaper articles (168.3 million words) and was tested on 67,216 articles it had never seen (20.0 million words).

Every layout is **best found** (RESEARCH_SPEC section 14). The Wikipedia-trained version of style D was certified within 4.19 % of the lowest J1 any layout of its scope can reach (Phase 4 lower bound 1.7578); this layout, searched on Wikipedia + news, was not re-certified. It has the same base layer as that certified layout. The Wikipedia-trained version of style A was certified within 4.31 % of the lowest J1 any layout of its scope can reach (Phase 4 lower bound 1.7869); this layout, searched on Wikipedia + news, was not re-certified.

## Kala Goti (Kala গতি): style A, UniJoy-style vowels

* Every vowel sign (kar) has its own key: ক + ি = কি.
* A full vowel is hasant (K) and then its sign: ্ + ি = ই, ্ + া = আ (as in UniJoy).
* অ is Shift+F.
* Hasant also joins consonants: ক + ্ + ষ = ক্ষ.

## Kala Jhor (Kala ঝড়): style D, context vowels

* One key per vowel; Kala picks its form from the letter before it.
* After a consonant it gives the sign (kar): ক + ি = কি.
* Anywhere else it gives the full vowel: at the start of a word ি = ই; after a vowel া + ি = আই.
* Press the same vowel key twice after a consonant for the full vowel: ব + ি + ি = বই.
* Three presses give sign + vowel: ত ি ন ি ি ি = তিনিই.
* অ is Shift+L; hasant (C) joins consonants: ক + ্ + ষ = ক্ষ.

## Verification

Both files pass a port of Kala's import checks (`NativeLayoutReader`) and every label sits on its research key. Kala Jhor has no sequences: its vowel rule is described in `about.style.typingRule` for Kala to implement. For Kala Goti a port of Kala's fixed-layout engine (`FixedLayoutManager`) types, from a fresh word, the research encoder's keystrokes of the most frequent word types of each held-out text (words that need a key outside v1, such as ZWNJ, — or Latin letters, are skipped):
