# Observed pilot checklist

Use Kala's existing Learn → research skill check, first with consenting internal testers. Keep every row pending until observed on the exact installed build. Local HTTP and unit-test evidence is recorded separately in validation.json.

1. Freeze the installed Release artifact hash and code revision/source hashes, protocol 2026.10-literature-r5, bank 1.4.0, preparation/selection policy, mapping hash, style and engine/capture-v2 identity. Keep the public prompt bank in its original versioned Kala source; record its hash here. Do not mix banks/builds in the initial cohort.
2. Record the physical external and laptop keyboard checks: left/right Shift down/up, held/repeated Shift, punctuation, number row and ISO keys. Compare raw tuples, position labels and modifier state. Keep raw recordings unchanged. Unknown positions need an explicit exclusion.
3. Observe a complete 4:20 check. With explicit sharing consent, compare the pre-upload compressed-byte SHA-256 with receiver and published package bytes. Run the independent audit and score verifier on that exact package.
4. Exercise fast composition/replacements, native Backspace/Delete, selections and delayed notifications. Record source/link outcomes. Exact key links must never imply known delivery. Unknown attribution stays reason-coded and excluded locally.
5. Observe pause/resume, focus loss, timed Enter/timeout boundaries, repeats, stop and interruption. Check that external/private typing is not captured and no motor interval crosses a pause/prompt boundary. Preserve unfinished text and the original time denominator.
6. Decline sharing: retain a usable local result and verify that no upload/public package appears.
7. Consent to sharing while offline. Restart the client, reconnect, and verify one stable session, compressed hash and receipt. Restart the receiver between retries and verify stable idempotence.
8. With a disposable consenting identity and queued data, withdraw. Verify local receiver cleanup and removal from the current public packages/manifest. Retry queued uploads and confirm they cannot republish. Rebuild current analyses and confirm the contributor disappears. Earlier Git history/copies may retain packages, as the dataset README explains.
9. Observe ordinary Bangla composition and enabled corrections in representative external apps; this is separate from the research editor and synthetic adapter tests. Record app/build, keyboard and focus/edit transitions without private text.
10. Review all failures and exclusions, then run a small varied pilot before broader familiar-layout collection. The larger proposed cohort is 20 contributors × at least two sessions each; use pilot variance and contributor-level simulation for later precision planning.

For every scenario record: date/time (Asia/Dhaka), observer, installed build hash, OS, keyboard/geometry, fixed mapping hash/style/engine, protocol/bank, result, package/receipt/evidence hashes and exclusion reason. A test result is not an observed_pass. Do not enter credentials or deletion secrets.

Only clear the pilot gate when all required scenarios have observed evidence, unexplained text/key losses are resolved, byte identities and independent scores match, and remaining warnings have tested recovery or predefined exclusions. Recheck affected scenarios after capture changes. Do not publish an empirical layout score until its complete operation/context support and transfer validation are available.
