## 4. Search (task 4)

Code: `bangla_dh.search.anneal`. Per style: a quadratic assignment of 52 labels to 52 slots with the fixed keys folded into a linear term, scored with O(1) swap deltas. Each run: simulated annealing over pair swaps (base↔Shift included), geometric cooling from T0 (mean uphill delta of 2,000 random swaps / ln 2) to T0 × 0.001, 1,000,000 steps; steepest descent to a swap-local optimum; 1,000 iterations of iterated local search (perturbation by 2–6 random pair swaps or whole-key exchanges). 64 seeds per style; the first seed starts from the frequency-sorted greedy layout, the others from random permutations. Frequencies: bnwiki/train.

## 9. Findings and points for the owner

* **Style ranking (held-out J1):** D < A < B < C. D's lead over A (0.0332) comes from E1 (0.0319): A types every independent vowel as two keys (1.0385 against 1.0020 keystrokes per non-space unit). C is far behind (0.3187 more than D) because every vowel sign (about 27 % of non-space units) costs two keystrokes.
* **Against UniJoy:** UniJoy's J1 2.8084 splits into E1 1.7718, E2 0.4023 (same-finger 0.4043) and M 0.6342; style D has E1 1.5081, E2 0.0351 (same-finger 0.0466) and M 0.2911. Most of the gain is fewer same-finger pairs and fewer Shift presses; UniJoy was never optimized for this model, so the comparison measures the model's preference, not typing speed.
* **Held-out stability:** every best layout scores the same on held-out Wikipedia text as on the training split (section 6), so the search did not overfit the training frequencies. Both splits are encyclopedic text (D4).
* **Rounding:** the one-decimal grid lowers every J1 by about 0.006–0.011 and keeps the order of the styles and UniJoy.
* **Collisions of the repeat rules.** C cannot type the same independent vowel twice (এএফসি; its three-press rule is not defined yet); A and D cannot type hasant + vowel sign, and B and D cannot type a doubled sign or an independent vowel followed by its own sign (typing errors in the corpus). B and D type তিনিই with three presses (owner rule of 10 October 2026). Rule escapes on the held-out split: A 6 (0.0000 %), B 352 (0.0008 %), C 2,055 (0.0046 %), D 1,419 (0.0032 %). They cost nothing here; even at 3 keystrokes each on the most expensive slot with the worst penalty, they could add at most A 0.0000, B 0.0003, C 0.0016, D 0.0011 to J1, which does not change the ranking.
* **D3, fingering.** All layouts are optimized for the model-optimal ANSI fingering (for example B is a right-index key and Z a left-ring key in that grid). UniJoy's typists use traditional fingering, under which UniJoy scores slightly worse (section 3). Whether Bangla-DH should assume angle-mod-like fingering is still D3.
* **D2 check.** M is 15–16 % of every best layout's J1. It assumes one Shift press per shifted symbol and no bigram penalty for Shift; the planned typing-time measurement should test both, because the search places rare symbols on Shift exactly as M prices them.
* **Phase 4:** the elementary bound leaves a gap of about 10–11 % (section 8). Phase 4 certifies these layouts as bounded near-optima with a gap of 4.0–4.3 % and proves them optimal in stated neighbourhoods ([../phase4/REPORT.md](../phase4/REPORT.md)).
